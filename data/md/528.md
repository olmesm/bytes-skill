### Deno Joins Cloudflare

I woke up this morning ready to do the mental gymnastics necessary to write a story about Theo’s $420k TypeScript slopfork, so when I saw [Cloudflare announce that they are acquiring Deno](https://blog.cloudflare.com/deno-joins-cloudflare/), my first thought was THANK G\*SH.

My second thought was, did we call this? Back in August when Ry released his project `celld`, [we made the *observation*](https://bytes.dev/archives/511) that this was a not-so-subtle message in a bottle asking for Cloudflare to acquire Deno. And it turns out we were right… finally.

**Why now?** Like the rest of the [ZIRP-era VC-backed open-source projects](https://bytes.dev/archives/493), Deno was burning cash with little to no hope of raising another round of funding, so they had to find a buyer. Fortunately, Cloudflare also had a problem to solve. They needed a better off-ramp for their platform to stop scaring away enterprise customers who were wary of vendor lock-in.

But as much as we’re happy for Ry, Bert and co, none of us are getting any Cloudflare stock. So what are we all getting out of this deal?

**What developers can expect moving forward:**

- **A workerd Rust rewrite:** `workerd`, the open-source runtime that powers Cloudflare’s Workers platform, is getting rewritten in Rust. Is this a good thing? “Who cares it’s Rust!” - DHH (probably).
- **A Durable Object escape hatch:** `celld` accomplished what Cloudflare has struggled to do up to this point, which was to make the Durable Object primitive self-hostable. Ry and Bert’s main objective is to continue to build “first-class” support for self-hosting `workerd`.
- **A future for Deno (the runtime):** Despite being open source, the majority of the contributions to the project have been made by Deno (the company) employees. With this acquisition, we no longer need to ask whether the runtime can transition to being a community-led effort… at least for the next year.

**Bottom line:** The only metric that really matters is shots on goal. If you’re us, it might take you 1000 hot takes to finally nail a prediction, or if you’re Ry, a single love letter to secure the partner of your dreams. Either way, shoot your shot, fellas (and ladies).
